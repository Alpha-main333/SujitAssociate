Privacy Notice

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Sujit Associate respects your privacy. This notice explains what information may be collected when you visit this website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information you choose to share

When you send us an enquiry, we may receive the information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, project details, and the contents of your message. You can also contact us directly by phone or email.

Please share only information that is relevant to your enquiry. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive personal information through this website.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to understand and respond to your enquiry, communicate with you, and consider or provide the services you ask about. We may also use relevant records to manage our business and meet legal obligations.

We do not use enquiry details to send unrelated marketing messages. If our practices change, we will update this notice as appropriate.

Service providers and external content

We may rely on service providers to help us receive and manage enquiries or to display features such as the map on our contact page. Those providers may process limited information, such as details you submit or technical information about your visit, to provide their services.

External services are responsible for their own privacy practices. Their handling of information may be described in their privacy notices. You can choose not to use an optional external feature and contact us directly instead.

Cookies and technical information

When you visit a website, your browser may share basic technical information needed to load and display pages. Features supplied by external providers may use their own cookies or similar technologies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; some features may not work as expected if they are blocked.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage our business relationship, maintain appropriate records, and meet applicable legal requirements. The time period may depend on the nature of the enquiry and any ongoing work.

How we protect information

We take reasonable steps to protect information shared with us from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. No method of sending or storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have shared, or to request that it be corrected or deleted. We may need to retain some information where required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

If you prefer not to use the enquiry form, you can contact us by phone or email using the details on the contact section.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for people seeking information about our services. It is not designed to collect personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe a child has shared personal information with us through this website.

Updates to this notice

We may update this notice to reflect changes to our website, services, or privacy practices. The latest version will appear on this page with its updated date.
