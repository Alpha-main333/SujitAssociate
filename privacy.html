Privacy notice

This notice describes what happens to information you choose to share through this website and the third-party services it uses.

Information you provide

If you send a project enquiry, the form asks for your name, email address, project subject and message. A phone number is optional. Please do not include payment information, passwords, or other sensitive personal details.

How enquiries are handled

The contact form sends the details you submit to Sujit Associate using FormSubmit so the business can respond to your enquiry. The website itself does not operate a separate enquiry database. FormSubmit states in its documentation that submissions are retained in its archive for 30 days. Its spam protection may present a CAPTCHA.

If you prefer not to use the form, contact Sujit Associate directly by phone or email using the details on the contact section.

Embedded map

The contact section embeds a Google Map. Loading that map connects your browser to Google, which may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser data. See Google’s privacy policy for details.

Storage and your choices

This website does not add analytics or advertising tracking. FormSubmit’s spam-protection CAPTCHA and the embedded map are operated by third parties and may use their own technologies. You can avoid the form and map and contact the business directly instead.

For a question about an enquiry you submitted, or to request correction or deletion, contact Sujit Associate. Requests may be subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.
